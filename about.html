About Processing Community Day India

Processing Community Day India is created by and for local communities across the country.

Processing Community Day celebrates art, code, and diversity through talks, workshops, performances, and collaborative making. The initiative supports open learning and welcomes participants from different backgrounds and skill levels.

Across cities and online editions, the community brings together artists, designers, coders, educators, and curious learners. The goal is to make creative coding more accessible, inclusive, and locally connected.
