Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community experience.

All participants in Processing Community Day India, including attendees, speakers, artists, performers, workshop leaders, volunteers, organizers, and partners, are expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary behavior in any form. This includes behavior based on gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, age, race, ethnicity, nationality, language, religion, or any other personal characteristic.

If someone engages in unacceptable behavior, organizers may take action including warnings, removal from activities, or exclusion from future events.

To report concerns, message us on Instagram: @processingindia or @ccsantein.
